Ludo Mahal – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Ludo Mahal ("the game") is an Android game made by Kelvoro Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and what we do with it. The game is open to players of all ages, including children.

1. The short version

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Your profile and game progress are stored on your own device.
  • Children under 13 only see ads suitable for children, with no personalised advertising.
  • You can delete your data at any time inside the game.

2. Information the game uses

Profile and progress (stored on your device). When you start the game you choose a username and avatar and enter your date of birth. We use the date of birth only to work out your age group so that the game can apply the right privacy and advertising settings. Your username, avatar, age group, coins, levels, unlocked items, settings and match history are saved in a file on your device. We do not receive a copy of this file.

Playing as a guest. You can play as a guest. Players under 13 play as guests only and cannot sign in with an account.

Google Play Games (optional, ages 13 and over). If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, Google provides the game with a player identifier and display name so your progress can be linked to your Google account. This is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising

The game may show ads, including optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch to earn coins or unlock items. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect device information such as your advertising ID, IP address and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads.

  • Under 13: ads are set as child-directed. No personalised ads are shown and no personalised advertising data is used.
  • 13 to 17: only non-personalised ads are shown.
  • 18 and over: personalised ads may be shown. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) we ask for your consent first, and you can change your choice later.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent personal information.
  • We do not ask for your real name, address, phone number, photos, contacts or location.
  • We do not use chat or voice features. The game has no way for players to message each other.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is described in this policy.

5. Children

Ludo Mahal is designed to be suitable for children. For players under 13 the game uses a date of birth only to set the age group, keeps all profile information on the device, does not allow account sign-in, and shows only child-directed ads. Parents and guardians who have questions or want information removed can contact us using the details below.

6. Data sharing

We share information only with the service providers needed to run the game, namely Google (Google Play, Google Play Games and AdMob), and only as described above. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Your profile is kept on your device until you delete it. You can delete everything the game has saved by going to Settings > Delete my data in the game, or by uninstalling the game. If you used Google Play Games, you can manage that data in your Google account.

8. Security

Because your profile stays on your device, it is protected by your device's own security. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we keep the information we handle to a minimum.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game changes, for example when online features are added. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. Significant changes will also be shown in the game.
